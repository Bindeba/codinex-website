Overview

Practical technology, priced for real businesses

Codinex Computers Limited was founded in Mbarara in 2012 by a team of dynamic IT professionals and computer engineers with a practical approach to customer service.

We manage large-scale IT projects while staying hands-on with service and maintenance of everyday equipment — because our priority is finding you smart, affordable technology and custom software that fits your business.

Today we connect with thousands of customers every year, with more than 1,000 clients enjoying our networking & security, software development, and hardware repair & maintenance services across East Africa.
